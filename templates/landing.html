FAQ

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by our configured payment provider. The payment options available to you are shown by the provider and depend on the merchant account. Payment is confirmed by the provider before your order or plan is marked paid.

When does my plan start?

After the payment provider confirms your payment to our server. Confirmation timing depends on the payment method and provider.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. You renew manually from your dashboard and the new period is added after your current expiry date, so you never lose paid days.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Every paid order has a printable invoice in your dashboard.
